About

The crew behind the ridez

Freewill Ridez is a motorcycle crew and content brand out of Pontiac, Michigan. What started as a handful of sportbikes meeting up turned into one of the most consistent group rides in the state — 75+ bikes showing up, eight months straight, staging out of downtown Pontiac and rolling everywhere from the Detroit streets to the Tunnel of Trees up north.

It's a movement: no drama, everybody rides home. The rides are announced in the FREEWILLRIDEZ GROUP RIDEZ Facebook group — meet-ups, routes, and ride info all land there first — and the riding itself lives on TikTok at @freewillflow.

Then there's the line: numbered volume drops — For The Culture, Till Death Do Us Part, Detroit Motor City, Living Ghost, King of the Streets — plus the Truly Wanted and Public Enemy Number One drops. Small runs, printed heavy, and they sell out at the rides. Vol. 1 went 40 for 40. Michigan Central Station keeps showing up in the artwork for a reason: it's home turf.
